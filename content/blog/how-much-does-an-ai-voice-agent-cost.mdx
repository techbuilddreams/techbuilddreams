If you're a small-business owner pricing out an AI voice agent in 2026, you've probably found two extremes: free DIY no-code tutorials that produce robotic, frustrating bots, and enterprise quotes north of $50,000 a year. Most of our small-business clients land somewhere in the middle. Here's the actual math.

## The two cost components

Every AI voice agent has two billable parts:

1. **Setup** — design, voice configuration, integrations (CRM, calendar, SMS), and testing. One-time.
2. **Usage** — what you pay every month for inference, voice generation, and telephony.

If a vendor quotes you a single all-in number with no breakdown, that's a yellow flag. The setup is real work; the usage is metered, and you should be able to see both.

## Realistic setup ranges

| Agent complexity | Setup cost | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single-intent screener | $3,000 – $5,000 | One purpose (e.g., "qualify roadside calls"), no integrations |
| Multi-intent agent | $5,000 – $10,000 | Handles 3-5 call types, basic CRM integration |
| Full booking agent | $10,000 – $20,000 | Multi-intent, calendar booking, CRM, SMS follow-up, bilingual |
| Custom enterprise | $20,000+ | Multiple phone numbers, complex routing, deep integrations |

## Realistic monthly usage

For a typical service-business agent answering 200-500 calls per month, expect **$0.10–$0.40 per minute** depending on the voice quality tier you pick and the underlying provider. Most small-business clients land in the **$200–$800/month** range.

The big variable is voice quality. OpenAI Realtime, ElevenLabs Turbo, and Cartesia Sonic are all roughly in that price band; cheaper TTS providers exist but the conversation feels noticeably worse.

## What drives the price up

- **Bilingual support** — adds a small inference cost but more setup time
- **CRM and calendar integrations** — usually one-time setup, no ongoing cost
- **Call recording and transcription** — ~$0.01 per minute extra
- **High call volume** (1,000+/month) — usage scales linearly, no per-seat fee
- **Premium voice tiers** — ElevenLabs &gt; OpenAI Realtime &gt; cheaper TTS

## What does *not* drive price

- "Number of users" — this isn't a SaaS seat license
- "Number of phone numbers" — typically a few dollars per number per month
- "AI training" — you're not training a custom model; you're configuring prompts
